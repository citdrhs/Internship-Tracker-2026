Unfortunately, this app cannot confirm these emails actually reached each mentor.

Please log into the noreply@drhscit.org mailbox in Zoho Mail and check for three common things:

  • The Sent folder, to confirm each message actually went out.
  • The Inbox for a bounce-back ("Undelivered Mail Returned to Sender"), which usually means the address was mistyped (you can add a new email to the organization later)
  • The Inbox for an auto-reply / vacation message. Take note of these so you know the mentor is away and can follow up when they return.

Onboarding email could not be sent

The email account noreply@drhscit.org rejected login, so the onboarding email(s) were not sent. This is almost always a wrong or expired email password, or the mail server being temporarily unavailable.

Please contact a CIT system administrator to resolve this issue, then try again.
